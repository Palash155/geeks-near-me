# Geeks Near Me — Services Page SEO Content

## Document purpose

This is the review-ready content draft for the future `/services` landing page. It is written for Sydney households, home offices and small businesses that need practical help but may not know the technical name of the problem.

The page is a service hub, not a replacement for the homepage or the individual service pages. Its job is to help a visitor recognise their problem, choose the closest service and open the appointment request with the right service preselected.

## Content rules

- Market: Sydney, Australia
- Language: Australian English
- Primary CTA: **Request an Appointment**
- Secondary CTA: **Call 0403 171 348**
- Never imply that an appointment is instantly booked or guaranteed
- Never add unverified pricing, reviews, statistics, certifications or guaranteed response times
- Do not claim exact suburb coverage until the client approves a service-area list
- Keep the main content visible in semantic HTML; animation is presentation only
- Link every service to its own descriptive landing page when those routes are published

## 7. What to include in your request

**Eyebrow:** HELP US UNDERSTAND THE ISSUE

## A few useful details can make the next step clearer

You do not need to write a technical report. When possible, include:

- What you were trying to do when the problem appeared
- What you can see on the screen or device
- When the issue started and whether it happens every time
- Which devices, applications or accounts are affected
- Anything you have already tried
- A relevant screenshot or photo, if available
- Your suburb and postcode for an on-site request

Never include passwords, banking details, one-time security codes or other sensitive login information in the form or an uploaded image.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

**Eyebrow:** COMMON QUESTIONS

## Before you request IT support

### Which IT service should I choose?

Choose the service that seems closest to the problem. If the cause is unclear or several devices are affected, choose **Something else** and describe what is happening. You do not need to diagnose the issue yourself.

### Can I request both remote and on-site IT support?

The form asks you to choose a preferred support type for the current request. Our team reviews the issue and may recommend a different approach if it is more practical.

### Do you provide IT support for both homes and small businesses?

Yes. Appointment requests are accepted from Sydney households, home offices and small businesses for the services listed on this page.

### Can you help if several technology problems are connected?

Yes. Describe the devices and issues involved in one request. Our team can review whether they are likely to be handled together or whether separate work should be discussed.

### Do I need to know the make and model of my device?

Not always, but including the device type and any visible model information can help us understand the request. Do not delay submitting the form if those details are difficult to find.

### Is the preferred appointment time guaranteed?

No. The selected date and time are your preferences. The appointment is confirmed only after Geeks Near Me reviews the request and contacts you.

### Can I upload a screenshot or photo?

Yes. A relevant photo, screenshot or supported document can be added when it helps explain the problem. Do not upload passwords, banking information, security codes or unrelated personal documents.

### How do I know whether an on-site visit is available in my suburb?

Add your suburb and postcode to the appointment request. Availability is reviewed using the location, service type and scheduling requirements before the appointment is confirmed.

## Structured-data and implementation notes

- Use `BreadcrumbList` for Home → IT Support Services
- Keep the eight services in visible semantic HTML and use a real crawlable `<a href>` for every service route
- Sitewide `LocalBusiness` data should include only client-confirmed business facts
- The page can use an `ItemList` to describe the visible service directory, provided the JSON-LD matches the rendered content
- Keep all FAQ answers visible in the HTML
- `FAQPage` markup should not be treated as a rich-result strategy for this business; Google currently limits regular FAQ rich results mainly to well-known government and health websites
- Add the canonical URL only after the production domain is confirmed
- Include `/services` and every published service route in the XML sitemap
- Use one clear H1 and preserve the H2/H3 hierarchy above
